




_Step.TTFS
==========


.. py:method:: biophysics_fitting.hay.evaluation._Step.TTFS(voltage_traces)


   Get the time to first spike in the somatic voltage trace.

   :param voltage_traces: dictionary with the voltage traces of the soma and dendrite.
   :type voltage_traces: dict

   :returns:

             Dictionary mapping ``".check_1AP"`` to whether there is at least one action potential in the somatic voltage trace,
                 and ``".raw"`` to the time to first spike in the somatic voltage trace.
   :rtype: dict

   .. seealso:: :py:meth:`~biophysics_fitting.ephys.STEP_time_to_first_spike`
