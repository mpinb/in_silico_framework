.. function:: biophysics_fitting.hay.evaluation._Step.AI(voltage_traces)


   Get the adaptation index of the somatic voltage trace.

   :param voltage_traces: dictionary with the voltage traces of the soma and dendrite.
   :type voltage_traces: dict

   :returns:

             Dictionary mapping ``".check_2ISI"`` to whether there are 2 action potentials in the somatic voltage trace,
                 and ``".raw"`` to the adaptation index of the somatic voltage trace.
   :rtype: dict

   .. seealso:: :func:`~biophysics_fitting.ephys.STEP_adaptation_index`
