

APh
===


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. function:: biophysics_fitting.hay.evaluation._Step.APh(voltage_traces)


   Get the AP heights fo all APs in the somatic voltage trace.

   :param voltage_traces: dictionary with the voltage traces of the soma and dendrite.
   :type voltage_traces: dict

   :returns:

             Dictionary mapping ``".check_1AP"`` to whether there is at least one action potential in the somatic voltage trace,
                 and ``".raw"`` to the AP heights in the somatic voltage trace.
   :rtype: dict

   .. seealso:: :func:`~biophysics_fitting.ephys.AP_height`
