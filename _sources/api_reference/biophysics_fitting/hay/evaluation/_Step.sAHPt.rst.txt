

sAHPt
=====


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. function:: biophysics_fitting.hay.evaluation._Step.sAHPt(voltage_traces)


   Get the slow afterhyperpolarization time in the somatic voltage trace.

   :param voltage_traces: dictionary with the voltage traces of the soma and dendrite.
   :type voltage_traces: dict

   :returns:

             Dictionary mapping ``".check_2AP"`` to whether there are 2 action potentials in the somatic voltage trace,
                 and ``".raw"`` to the slow afterhyperpolarization time in the somatic voltage trace.
   :rtype: dict
