




_Step.sAHPd
===========


.. py:method:: biophysics_fitting.hay.evaluation._Step.sAHPd(voltage_traces)


   Get the slow afterhyperpolarization depth in the somatic voltage trace.

   :param voltage_traces: dictionary with the voltage traces of the soma and dendrite.
   :type voltage_traces: dict

   :returns:

             Dictionary mapping ``".check_2AP"`` to whether there are 2 action potentials in the somatic voltage trace,
                 and ``".raw"`` to the slow afterhyperpolarization depth in the somatic voltage trace.
   :rtype: dict

   .. seealso:: :py:meth:`~biophysics_fitting.ephys.STEP_slow_ahp_depth`
