

setup_soma_step
===============


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. py:function:: biophysics_fitting.setup_stim.setup_soma_step(cell, amplitude=None, delay=None, duration=None, dist=0)


   Setup up a step current at the soma, or a given :paramref:`dist` from the soma.

   :param cell: The cell object.
   :type cell: ~single_cell_parser.cell.Cell
   :param amplitude: The amplitude of the step current.
   :type amplitude: float
   :param delay: The delay of the step current.
   :type delay: float
   :param duration: The duration of the step current.
   :type duration: float
   :param dist: The distance from the soma to the recording/injection site.
   :type dist: float

   :returns: None. Adds a step current pipette to the cell under the name 'iclamp'.
