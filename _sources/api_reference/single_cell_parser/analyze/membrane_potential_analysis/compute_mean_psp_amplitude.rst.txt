

compute_mean_psp_amplitude
==========================


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. py:function:: single_cell_parser.analyze.membrane_potential_analysis.compute_mean_psp_amplitude(vTraces, tStim, dt, width=35.0, t_delay=15.0)


   Compute the mean amplitude of all PSPs across multiple voltage traces.

   The post-synaptic potential (PSP) amplitude is the maximum membrane voltage deflection
   between :paramref:`tStim` + :paramref:`t_delay` and :paramref:`tStim` + :paramref:`delay` + :paramref:`width`.

   :param vTraces: List of voltage traces.
   :type vTraces: list
   :param tStim: Timepoint of stimulation (in ms).
   :type tStim: float
   :param dt: Time step of the voltage traces.
   :type dt: float
   :param width: Width of the PSP window. Default is :math:`35.0 ms`.
   :type width: float
   :param t_delay: Delay of the PSP window (i.e. time between the stimulus and the PSP onset). Default is :math:`15.0 ms`.
   :type t_delay: float

   :returns: A tuple containing the delay and the mean amplitude of the PSPs.
   :rtype: tuple
