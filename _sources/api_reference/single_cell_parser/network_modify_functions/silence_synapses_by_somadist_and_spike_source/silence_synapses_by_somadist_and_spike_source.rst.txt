

silence_synapses_by_somadist_and_spike_source
=============================================


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. py:function:: single_cell_parser.network_modify_functions.silence_synapses_by_somadist_and_spike_source.silence_synapses_by_somadist_and_spike_source(cell, evokedNW, soma_dist_ranges=None, spike_sources=None)


   Silences synapse activation at a :paramref:`soma_dist_ranges`,
   that are from presynaptic origins **not** listed in :paramref:`spike_sources`.

   :param cell: The cell to modify.
   :type cell: single_cell_parser.cell.Cell
   :param soma_dist_ranges: A dictionary with synapse types as keys and
                            tuples of minimum and maximum soma distances as values.
   :type soma_dist_ranges: dict
   :param spike_sources: A list of spike sources to keep active.
   :type spike_sources: list
