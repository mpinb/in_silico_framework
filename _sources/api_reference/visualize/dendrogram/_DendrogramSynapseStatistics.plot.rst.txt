.. function:: visualize.dendrogram._DendrogramSynapseStatistics.plot(ax=None)


   Plot the synapse statistics

   Plots out a histogram of the total amount of synapses per bin of soma distance.

   :param ax: The matplotlib axes object. Default is ``None`` and a new figure is created.
   :type ax: matplotlib.axes.Axes

   :returns: The matplotlib axes object.
   :rtype: :class:`matplotlib.axes.Axes`
