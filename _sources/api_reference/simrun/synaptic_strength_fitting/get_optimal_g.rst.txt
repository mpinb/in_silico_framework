

get_optimal_g
=============


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. function:: simrun.synaptic_strength_fitting.get_optimal_g(self, measured_data, method='dynamic_baseline', threashold=0.1)


   Calculate the optimal synaptic conductance such that the EPSP matches empirical data.

   For each celltype (or merged celltype), the optimal synaptic conductance is calculated
   by linearly interpolating the relationship between the synaptic strength and each of the EPSP statistics (mean, median and maximum).
   This linear interpolation is cross-referenced with empirically observed statistics to infer the optimal synaptic conductance.

   :param measured_data: A table containing the empirical EPSP statistics (mean, median and maximum) for each celltype.
                         Must contain the keys: ``[EPSP_mean_measured, EPSP_median_measured, EPSP_max_measured]``.
   :type measured_data: pd.DataFrame
   :param method: ``dynamic_baseline`` or ``constant_baseline``.
   :type method: str
   :param threashold: The threashold to consider a celltype as excitatory.
   :type threashold: float

   :returns: A table of the optimal synaptic conductance for each celltype.
   :rtype: pd.DataFrame

   .. seealso:: :py:meth:`~simrun.synaptic_strength_fitting.calculate_optimal_g`.
