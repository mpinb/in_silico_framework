

get_synapse_coordinates
=======================


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. function:: simrun.synaptic_strength_fitting.PSPs.get_synapse_coordinates(population, flatten=False, cell_indices=None)


   Get the coordinates of all synapses of a particular celltype.

   :param population: The celltype to get the synapse coordinates from.
   :type population: str
   :param flatten: Whether to flatten the list of synapse coordinates. Default: ``False``.
   :type flatten: bool
   :param cell_indices: A list of cell indices to get the synapse coordinates from. Default: ``None`` (all synapses).
   :type cell_indices: list

   :returns: A list of synapse coordinates.
   :rtype: list
