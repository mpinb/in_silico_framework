




PSPs.get_synapse_coordinates_with_psp_amplitude
===============================================


.. py:method:: simrun.synaptic_strength_fitting.PSPs.get_synapse_coordinates_with_psp_amplitude(population, g=1.0, merged=True, select_synapses_per_cell=None)


   Get the synapse coordinates and the PSP amplitude for each synapse.

   :param population: The celltype to get the synapse coordinates for.
   :type population: str
   :param g: The synaptic strength value to get the PSP amplitude for.
   :type g: float
   :param merged: Whether to merge the voltage traces of cell types.
   :type merged: bool
   :param select_synapses_per_cell: The amount of synapses per cell to select. Default: ``None`` (all synapses).
   :type select_synapses_per_cell: int

   :returns: An array of synapse coordinates and PSP amplitudes.
   :rtype: np.array

   .. seealso::

      :py:meth:`~simrun.synaptic_strength_fitting.merge_celltypes` for merging
      the voltage traces of certain cell types.
