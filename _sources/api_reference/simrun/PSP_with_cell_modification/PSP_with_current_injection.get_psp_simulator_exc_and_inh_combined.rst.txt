

get_psp_simulator_exc_and_inh_combined
======================================


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. function:: simrun.PSP_with_cell_modification.PSP_with_current_injection.get_psp_simulator_exc_and_inh_combined(gExRange=[1.0], mode='synapses')


   Set up and combine excitatory and inhibitory PSP simulators.

   This method initializes two PSPs objects, one for excitatory and one for inhibitory postsynaptic potentials,
   and combines them into a single PSPs object.

   :param mode: Mode of the simulation. Options:

                - ``'synapses'`` to activate individual synapses (default)
                - ``'cells'`` to activate individual cells
   :type mode: str
   :param gExRange: Range of excitatory conductance values to simulate (in :math:`\mu S`). Default: ``[1.0]``
   :type gExRange: list

   :returns: Object to simulate PSPs
   :rtype: :class:`~simrun.synaptic_strength_fitting.PSPs`
