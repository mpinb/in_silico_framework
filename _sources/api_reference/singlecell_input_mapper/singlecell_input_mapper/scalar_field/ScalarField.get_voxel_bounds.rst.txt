

get_voxel_bounds
================


.. Signature -------------------------------

.. function:: singlecell_input_mapper.singlecell_input_mapper.scalar_field.ScalarField.get_voxel_bounds(ijk)


   Gets the bounding box of voxel given by indices i,j,k.

   :param ijk: tuple of 3 integers: the indices of voxel in mesh.
   :type ijk: tuple

   .. warning:: Does not perform bounds checking. The voxel bounds may be beyond the span of the mesh.
